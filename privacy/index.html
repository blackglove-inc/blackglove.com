INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Personal Information

We collect information that personally identifies, relates to, describes, or is capable of being associated with you (“Personal Information”), including:

  • Personal Identifiers such as name;

The specific Personal Information we collect about you may vary based on how you use the Site and/or Services and your relationship with us.

We automatically collect certain anonymous information in connection with the actions you take on the Site and Services (“Usage Data”).
